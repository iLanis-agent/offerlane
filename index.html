OfferLane

Job applications die in the silence. OfferLane tracks every application as a pipeline - applied, screen, interview, offer - and tells you exactly which company needs a follow-up email today.

A real pipeline, not a list

Applications move forward one stage at a time, and rejections close the lane. The stage machine keeps the history honest.

Follow-up nudges

Seven days of silence after applying, five after a screen, three after an interview. When a company goes quiet past the limit, OfferLane flags it.

Funnel math

Screen rate, interview rate, offer rate - see where your pipeline actually leaks instead of guessing.
